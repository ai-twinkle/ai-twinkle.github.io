**Government Support for AI Through Open Data: Recognizing Excellence in Text Corpus Development**

The Ministry of Digital Affairs (moda) has officially presented its first "Outstanding Open Text Corpus Awards," marking a significant milestone in the government's open data policy to support artificial intelligence development. This initiative recognizes organizations that have demonstrated excellence in the public release and quality maintenance of text data, providing reliable and representative training materials for the developer community.

The establishment of this award reflects the government's commitment to the intersection of open data and AI development. By encouraging organizations to proactively release high-quality, legally licensed Traditional Chinese text datasets, this initiative helps improve the training efficiency and logical reasoning capabilities of local AI models. For development communities like Twinkle AI, which focus on model training and open-source technology, this represents a crucial resource for enhancing overall technical competitiveness.
